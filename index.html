What it does today

Search

Look up papers by topic, author, or university and lab across arXiv, Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex. Narrow by year and source. Coverage is focused on cs.DC, cs.SE, cs.AI and cs.AR.

Compare

Pick two papers and get a side-by-side table of bottlenecks, bounds, complexity ceilings and failure modes. Copy it as Markdown or print it.

Visualize

Turn a paper's system design into an interactive diagram you can drag and zoom, then export it as SVG or PNG.

Cite and reach out

Export BibTeX, IEEE or APA 7th. Draft a short email to an author or a lab, with placeholders left for you to personalize.

Where SWMP Labs fits

General research tools are strong at what they do. Elicit is good at structured extraction and screening, SciSpace at literature-review drafts and writing help, Consensus at quick evidence-backed answers, Undermind at deep citation-trail search. SWMP Labs is narrower on purpose.

  • Made for systems papers

    Most tools cover every field. We cover four arXiv categories and try to do those well.

  • Trade-offs, not only summaries

    The comparison view lines papers up on the things you argue about in a related-work section.

  • From reading to writing

    The place where you read a paper is also where you cite it and draft a note to its authors.

  • Public code

    The code is on GitHub, so you can see how it works.

This comparison is based on the tools' public descriptions as of October 2026. As far as we can tell, none of them puts a systems-focused comparison matrix, architecture diagrams and outreach drafting in one place. If we've missed something, please tell us.

Where Claude fits

We're building three parts of SWMP Labs around Claude, mostly because it reads long technical text carefully and writes in a natural voice.

  • Reading and organizing papers

    Turning a dense paper into its problem, bottleneck, assumptions and open questions, organized so it's easy to scan.

  • Writing to professors

    Short, specific cold emails tied to a paper or a lab, with placeholders so the researcher adds the personal details before sending.

  • Diagrams and summaries

    Clear architecture diagrams and short summaries that make a paper's design easy to grasp at a glance.

The prototype currently runs through a configurable model router, and we're integrating Claude for these three tasks. Model output is a draft. We aim to keep what a paper states separate from what the model infers, and we encourage checking anything important against the paper itself.

About

I'm Marawan, a cloud engineer. Systems research is spread across arXiv, Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex, and reading it usually means a lot of tabs, duplicate entries and notes that never quite get organized. SWMP Labs is my attempt to make that part easier, starting with the areas I know best: distributed systems, cloud infrastructure and AI systems. It's early, it's bootstrapped, and the code is public. If something breaks or feels off, I'd like to hear about it.
